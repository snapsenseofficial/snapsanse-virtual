# IG content plan (SnapSense planners & creators)

## Daily rhythm (Asia/Kuala_Lumpur)
| dept | story | feed |
|---|---|---|
| Authenticity | 08:30 | 09:30 |
| Awareness | 11:30 | 12:30 |
| Trust | 15:30 | 17:30 |
| Sales | 19:30 | 21:00 |

## Files (in `crew/data/igpack/`)
- `content/<dept>.json`: master plan per department (27 days). Malay captions plus hashtags (max 5).
- `content-en/<dept>.json`: the same plan with English poster text (copywriters).
- `jadual-posting.csv`: every post with date, time, dept, type, file and caption.
- `PANDUAN-AUTO-POST.md`: the owner's guide to scheduling in Meta Business Suite Planner.

## Planner tasks
1. Each new period, write 27 days per department. Follow `SCHEMA.md`. Layout mix: hook / tip / list / steps / faq / quote / price / dates / announce. Stories: poll / question / tip / announce / countdown.
2. Pillars:
   - Authenticity: behind the scenes, real stories.
   - Awareness: trends and hooks.
   - Trust: education, FAQ, portfolio.
   - Sales: packages and prices from the catalogue.
3. Key dates:
   - Deepavali: 8 Nov.
   - 11.11 and 12.12.
   - School holidays (Johor, Group B): 7–10 Nov and 5–31 Dec.
   - Wedding dates: `seed/wedding-dates.json`.
4. After the designer renders, update `jadual-posting.csv`. Then zip each week: the date folders, the contact sheet, the CSV and the guide.
5. Creators propose shoot ideas and pick which photos suit which day. Hand them to the editor (skill `photo-prep`).

## Rules
- Captions are Malay, with at most 2 emoji. Poster text is English.
- DM keywords are CAR, WEDDING and MOTORCYCLE.
- Never post directly. The owner schedules posts in Meta Business Suite, or approves them in the office.
